Admission Procedure

The common admission to 1st year Diploma courses for Government and Aided Polytechnics is conducted by the Department of Collegiate and Technical Education (DCTE), Karnataka.

Admission notifications are generally released after the SSLC results. Candidates can download the application form and admission brochure from the official DCTE website.

Admission to diploma courses is based on merit and reservation rules prescribed by the Government of Karnataka.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidate must be a citizen of India.
  • Candidate should have passed SSLC / 10th Standard or equivalent examination with minimum 35% marks.
  • Candidate should have studied in Karnataka for at least five academic years.
  • CBSE / ICSE / Other State students must produce Eligibility Certificate during admission.

Lateral Entry Admission

Candidates who have completed ITI (2-year course) or PUC II with required eligibility can apply for direct admission to the 3rd semester diploma course.
