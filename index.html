Welcome to Government Polytechnic Hubli

Government Polytechnic Hubli is a government institution established in 2009 under the Directorate of Technical Education, Karnataka. It offers diploma courses in engineering and focuses on practical and technical skills development.

Government Polytechnic Hubli is one of the leading technical institutions in Karnataka. It offers diploma courses in various engineering fields with a strong focus on practical learning and industry-oriented education.

Courses Offered

  • Computer Science Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Electronics & Communication Engineering
