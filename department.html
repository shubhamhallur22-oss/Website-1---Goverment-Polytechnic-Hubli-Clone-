Departments

Science Department

  • The Science Department strengthens the foundation of engineering students through Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • The department provides practical laboratory experience and conceptual understanding.
  • Students are encouraged to develop analytical and problem-solving skills.

Vision

  • To build a strong scientific foundation among engineering students.

Mission

  • To provide quality education in basic sciences.
  • To encourage scientific thinking and innovation.

Civil Engineering Department

  • The Department of Civil Engineering was one of the four branches established when Government Polytechnic Hubballi was founded in 2009.
  • The department has made significant academic progress by producing competent diploma graduates.
  • Many alumni have secured admissions in reputed engineering colleges and placements in companies.
  • Students actively participate in curricular and co-curricular activities.

Vision

  • Develop quality civil engineering professionals who serve ethically to create a healthy and sustainable society.

Mission

  • To develop skilled professionals through quality education and practical training.
  • To develop leadership qualities and entrepreneurial skills.
  • To encourage teamwork and social responsibility.

Computer Science Engineering Department

  • The department provides students with modern technical education in computer science.
  • Students gain knowledge in programming, networking, software development, and database systems.
  • The department supports academic growth and innovation among students and faculty.

Vision

  • To build a strong learning environment in Computer Science and Engineering.

Mission

  • To produce skilled computer science diploma graduates.
  • To provide state-of-the-art facilities for technical learning.
  • To encourage ethical values and leadership qualities.

Electrical and Electronics Engineering Department

  • The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering is one of the key branches of Government Polytechnic Hubballi.
  • The department provides quality education enriched with practical exposure.
  • The department is supported by highly experienced teaching and non-teaching staff members.
  • Students participate actively in academics, sports, and cultural activities.
  • Alumni of the department are placed in reputed companies and higher education institutions.

Vision

  • Create an environment to acquire skills through learning and practice to become effective technicians with ethical and environmental values.

Mission

  • Identification of relevant courses for skill development.
  • Providing practical learning with theoretical concepts.
  • Facilitating continuous evaluation and assessment.
  • Encouraging interdisciplinary learning and industrial exposure.

Mechanical Engineering Department

  • The Department of Mechanical Engineering was established in the year 2009.
  • The department provides quality education with practical exposure.
  • The department is facilitated by experienced teaching and non-teaching staff.
  • Students participate in academic, sports, and cultural activities.
  • Alumni are placed in reputed companies and many pursue higher education.

Vision

  • Development of academically excellent, culturally vibrant, socially responsible and globally competent human resources.

Mission

  • To keep pace with advancements in technology and knowledge.
  • To create a strong educational and moral foundation for students.
  • To achieve higher benchmarks of educational excellence.
