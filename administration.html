About Administration

The administration of Government Polytechnic Hubli ensures smooth academic, technical, and institutional functioning. The institution is committed to maintaining discipline, quality education, transparency, and student welfare.

Administrative Structure

  • Head of Departments (HODs)
  • Teaching Faculty
  • Office Superintendent
  • Administrative Staff
  • Lab Instructors
  • Library Staff

Principal

The Principal is the academic and administrative head of the institution. The administration works towards maintaining academic excellence, discipline, innovation, and student welfare.
