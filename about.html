About Government Polytechnic Hubli

Government Polytechnic Hubli was established in the year 2009 under the Department of Collegiate and Technical Education, Government of Karnataka. The institution is dedicated to providing quality technical education, practical knowledge, and skill development to students.

Located in Vidyanagar, Hubballi, the institution offers diploma courses in various engineering branches with modern laboratories, experienced faculty, and student-centered learning facilities.

Vision

To be an academic institution in dynamic equilibrium with social, ecological and economic environment striving continuously for excellence in education, research and technological service to the nation.

Mission

  • To offer state-of-the-art diploma programs.
  • To attract and develop talented and committed human resources and provide an environment conducive to innovation, creativity, team spirit and leadership.
  • To facilitate effective interactions among faculty and students and foster networking with alumni, industries and institutions.
  • To promote discipline, ethical values and social responsibility among students.

Institution Highlights

  • AICTE Approved Diploma Courses
  • Experienced Faculty Members
  • Modern Laboratories & Workshops
  • Placement Guidance & Career Support
  • Scholarship Facilities for Students
  • Sports, NSS & Cultural Activities
  • Eco-Friendly Campus Environment
